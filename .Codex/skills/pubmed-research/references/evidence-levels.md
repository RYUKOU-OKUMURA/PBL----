# Evidence Levels

Use this ranking when several papers are available for the same question.

## Level 1

- Systematic reviews
- Meta-analyses
- Guidelines grounded in systematic review methods

## Level 2

- Randomized controlled trials
- Randomized clinical trials

## Level 3

- Non-randomized controlled trials
- Prospective cohort studies
- Strong observational studies with a comparator

## Level 4

- Case-control studies
- Cross-sectional studies

## Level 5

- Case reports
- Expert opinion
- Editorials and narrative pieces

## Notes

- Prefer human studies over animal or in vitro studies for patient-facing content.
- Prefer more recent papers when quality is similar.
- A lower-level study can still matter if it covers a population or intervention that higher-level papers miss.
- Explicitly mention limitations when using Level 4 or Level 5 evidence in public-facing content.
